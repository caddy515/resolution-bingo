Privacy Policy

Resolution Bingo

Last updated: April 12, 2026

Resolution Bingo helps users create, track, and update a personal resolution bingo card. This Privacy Policy explains what information is used by the app and how it is handled.

Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the Resolution Bingo mobile app, web experience, and related services made available by the developer. By using the app, you acknowledge that your information may be processed as described here.

Information We Collect

Resolution Bingo uses Firebase services to support account access and cloud sync. The app may store:

  • Your email address for account creation, login, and password reset.
  • Your bingo card content, including card title, goals, categories, and progress.
  • Basic technical and security-related service data provided by Firebase.

How Information Is Used

  • To authenticate your account.
  • To sync your bingo card data across supported devices.
  • To save your progress and restore your card when you return.
  • To help maintain app security and reliability.

Service Providers

Resolution Bingo uses third-party infrastructure providers, including Google Firebase, to support authentication, cloud data storage, app functionality, and related security operations. Information processed through those services is subject to the applicable provider's systems and policies.

Disclosure

Resolution Bingo does not sell your personal information. Information may be disclosed when reasonably necessary to operate the service, comply with law, respond to legal process, protect users, investigate misuse, or protect the rights, property, or safety of the developer or others.

Snapshots and Sharing

The app allows you to create card snapshot images. If you choose to save or share a snapshot, that action is initiated by you and may place the image in your photo library or a third-party sharing destination you select.

You are solely responsible for the content you enter into the app and for any snapshot, export, or share action you choose to make. Please avoid storing or sharing sensitive personal information through card text or images.

Data Retention

Your card data remains associated with your account unless it is deleted by you or removed as part of account or service maintenance.

Data Access and Deletion

You can access and update your card information inside the app. If you want your account-associated data removed and cannot do so through the product experience, you may contact the developer using the contact details below and request deletion. Reasonable steps will be taken to process valid requests, subject to technical, legal, operational, and backup-related limits.

Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect account and card data, including Firebase Authentication, Firestore access rules, password protections, and related security controls. However, no method of transmission, storage, or electronic processing is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

International Processing

Depending on the infrastructure used to operate the service, your information may be processed or stored in jurisdictions outside your local region.

Children's Privacy

Resolution Bingo is not directed to children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Any updates will be reflected on this page with a revised effective date.

No Waiver of Rights

This Privacy Policy is provided for transparency about data practices and does not create any contractual promise beyond what is required by applicable law. Use of the app remains subject to the developer's rights to protect the service, investigate abuse, and take reasonable operational or security measures.
